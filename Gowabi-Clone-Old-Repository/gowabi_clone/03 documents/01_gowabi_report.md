# **GoWabi Analysis Report**

### **Title**
```
Leading app to book health, wellness, fitness services,
integrated with our SaaS/POS solution for thousands providers.
```


### **About**
```
GoWabi is the leading B2C and B2B application to find and book health, wellness, fitness,
beauty services in South-East Asia, supporting multiple payment options (Credit Card, Alipay, BNPL).
We offer a SaaS / CRM tool integrated with our POS for yearly subscription
at competitive prices in order to help optimize and manage your salon, spa, clinic, or studio.
```


### **GoWabi business models**
```
1. GoWabi offers a SaaS/CRM tool integrated with their POS for yearly subscriptions at competitive prices.
2. GoWabi takes a commission on each booking made through their platform
3. Businesses can pay for premium placement on GoWabi's website and app
4. GoWabi charges a fee for processing payments made through their platform
5. GoWabi can offer advanced data analytics and reporting services for an additional fee
```


### **How vendors earn benefits from GoWabi**
```
1. Vendors gain exposure to a large customer base.
2. The online booking system streamlines appointment scheduling,
   reducing administrative work and minimizing booking errors.
3. Offering various payment methods (Credit Card, Alipay, BNPL) makes it easier for customers to pay.
4.The integrated CRM tool helps vendors manage customer relationships, track service history, and personalize customer interactions.
5. The POS system simplifies sales transactions, links sales to inventory, and supports multiple payment methods, enhancing operational efficiency.
6. Tools for managing staff schedules, skills, and availability help vendors optimize their workforce and ensure smooth operations.
7. Tracking inventory levels and receiving alerts for low supplies prevent stock outs.
8. Access to comprehensive reports and analytics helps vendors understand business performance, identify trends, and make informed decisions.
9. Vendors can use the platform's marketing tools to promote their services, send targeted promotions, and engage with customers.
10. Customer reviews and feedback help vendors improve their services and build a positive reputation, attracting more customers.
```


### **How customers benefit from GoWabi**
```
1. Customers can easily find and book appointments online anytime, without needing to call or visit the salon or spa.
2. Access to a comprehensive list of services in one place makes it easier for customers to find what they need.
3. Multi-Payment Options.
4. Service Reviews and Ratings.
5. Personalized Recommendations.
6. Automated Reminders.
7. Promotional Offers
8. Easy Rescheduling and Cancellation
```
